# Output Template

## Full Science Check

```markdown
## Idea Tested
<neutral restatement>

## Bottom Line
<supported / plausible but unproven / mixed / weakly supported / contradicted / not enough information>

## Claim Check
| Claim | Verdict | Evidence | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <claim> | <verdict> | <source-backed summary> | <low/medium/high> |

## Mechanism
- <known mechanism or missing mechanism>

## Constraints and Failure Modes
- <physical, biological, technical, measurement, cost, scale, or adoption constraint>

## Counterevidence and Alternatives
- <simpler explanation, disconfirming result, conventional method, or substitute>

## Practical Implication
- <what the user can reasonably believe or try next>

## Next Test
- <small experiment, prototype, calculation, literature search, benchmark, or expert review>

## Falsifier
- <specific result that would change the verdict>

## Sources
- [Source](url): <what it supports>
```

## Quick Check

```markdown
## Verdict
<one paragraph>

## Why
- <mechanism/evidence>
- <constraint/caveat>

## Confidence
<low/medium/high because...>

## Sources
- [Source](url)
```

## Verdict Labels

- Supported: strong evidence and mechanism align.
- Plausible but unproven: reasonable mechanism, incomplete evidence.
- Mixed: credible evidence conflicts or applies to different conditions.
- Weakly supported: mostly indirect, anecdotal, or low-quality evidence.
- Contradicted: strong evidence or constraints argue against it.
- Not enough information: claim is too vague or evidence is missing.
